# 0. HARD VISUAL LOCKS — DO NOT CHANGE DURING THIS PHASE

The research phase is **not authorization to change CASTRO’S identity**.

The following are locked unless the user explicitly approves a future brand revision:

- **Instrument Serif** remains the editorial/public heading typeface.
- **Manrope** remains the body, navigation, form, control and operational typeface.
- The current CASTRO’S typography system is preserved; research may study hierarchy, scale, composition and kinetic use, but must not silently replace fonts.

- Existing product architecture, booking logic, backend contracts, roles/personas and real data are not redesign material.

- isolated 3D/spatial prototypes.

Research must **not** silently change:

# 2. REFERENCE — VEXEL

**Evidence:** `VERIFIED_IN_SOURCE` + `VERIFIED_VISUALLY` from supplied source/screens.

## What actually creates the VEXEL sense of continuity

- one persistent environmental background;
- a strong focal object in the hero;
- restrained ambient motion rather than movement everywhere;
- each product idea receives its own visual representation;
- content transitions attention through scroll;
- product concepts are demonstrated rather than only described;
- motion supports focus, progression and causality.

## Transferable principle

> Show the relationship/system instead of only describing it.

CASTRO’S adaptation examples:

- service → human context / consultation scene;
- training → active learning scene, session progression and media;
- spaces → spatial overview / photography / 360 / configuration;
- booking → date → loading → slots → selection → details → review.

## Reject

- dark-tech identity;
- blue/violet glow;
- glassmorphism system;
- orbitals;
- data particles as decoration;
- waveform motifs without real function;
- VEXEL metrics or claims;
- generic card reuse as the dominant architecture.

# 3. REFERENCE — LANDO NORRIS / OFF+BRAND

Sources:

- https://www.itsoffbrand.com/our-work/lando-norris
- https://landonorris.com/

**Evidence:** `VERIFIED_IN_SOURCE`.

OFF+BRAND explicitly identifies the project work as including Branding, Webflow, Development, Web Design, WebGL, 3D, Rive and Motion. Their published case study describes the objective as a high-performance digital platform that brings the person/world to life through immersive storytelling, dynamic interactions and responsive performance.

## Why it works

- the person is the experience anchor;
- motion expresses brand character rather than functioning as generic decoration;
- 3D/WebGL is connected to meaningful subject matter;
- content is organized as a world to explore, not a sequence of standard landing sections;
- media, typography and transitions operate as one authored system;
- performance is treated as part of the experience, with optimized delivery and lazy loading;
- responsive behavior is part of the design rather than an afterthought.

## Transferable principles for CASTRO’S

1. **Human anchor** — Elizabeth / real people / real space can become the visual center when approved assets exist.
2. **Identity-specific motion** — motion should communicate conversation, learning, spatial exploration and intentional progression rather than racing/speed.
3. **Signature object/space** — CASTRO’S can have a meaningful spatial anchor based on the real room instead of a decorative 3D object.
4. **World, not sections** — Home → Services → Training → Spaces → Booking should feel like one authored environment.
5. **Performance is design** — media/3D must include lazy loading, fallbacks, mobile strategy and reduced-motion behavior.

## Reject

- lime/black racing palette;
- motorsport visual language;
- speed for its own sake;
- 3D helmet analogy copied directly;
- aggressive transitions incompatible with CASTRO’S calm/institutional identity;
- any typography replacement inspired by Lando.

## Prototype question

Can CASTRO’S achieve a comparable sense of authored continuity using **real human media + editorial typography + one restrained spatial anchor**, without copying the visual identity or interaction intensity?

# 4. REFERENCE — OUSMANE DEMBÉLÉ / “NÉ POUR BRILLER”

Source:

- https://www.ousmaneballondor.fr/

**Evidence:** `VERIFIED_IN_SOURCE`.

The published site structures the experience as a story, moving from introductory identity into roots/history with archival imagery and chronological narrative.

## Why it works

- human history is the content architecture;
- real imagery carries emotional credibility;
- the visitor moves through a sequence with meaning, rather than browsing unrelated components;
- typography and photography work together as narrative material;
- the experience can be visually strong without requiring a software-dashboard metaphor.

## Transferable principles for CASTRO’S

- use real human evidence when available;
- founder/brand story can transition into services and training naturally;
- photography should be architectural, not an image placed inside a generic card;
- sequence can create emotional progression without scroll hijacking;
- a narrative can remain calm and institutional while still being memorable.

## Reject

- football-specific campaign language;
- “star/light” metaphors copied as identity;
- biography-first information architecture if it distracts from Castro’s service intent;
- any invented life story or unsupported business history.

## Prototype question

Can the CASTRO’S hero transition from Elizabeth/human presence into knowledge/services through a short, factual, media-led narrative without becoming a biography website?

# 5. REFERENCE — AROUNDA

Sources:

- https://arounda.agency/
- https://arounda.agency/case/smoothline
- https://arounda.agency/services/enterprise-website-design-agency

**Evidence:** `VERIFIED_IN_SOURCE`.

Useful recurring principles in Arounda’s own published process/case studies:

- research brand, audience and industry before design;
- define information architecture and user journey before high-fidelity UI;
- use authentic imagery to support trust;
- preserve brand alignment across typography, color and interaction;
- design responsive/mobile intentionally;
- use motion to support the experience rather than add arbitrary spectacle;
- prototype/test before final implementation.

## Transferable principles for CASTRO’S

- maintain brand system while evolving digital expression;
- storyboard/journey before implementation;
- use real human imagery as evidence and trust;
- mobile must be recomposed, not simply stacked;
- interaction should simplify choices and make progression obvious.

## Reject

- marketing metrics/case claims as Castro facts;
- generic agency/SaaS composition;
- importing another client’s palette or typography;
- card-heavy patterns when a scene or editorial composition is more appropriate.

Sources:

**Evidence:** `VERIFIED_IN_SOURCE` for Three.js capability; Castro-specific use remains `INFERRED` until prototyped.

## Castro-specific acceptable research uses

- simplified room/floor-plan overview;

## Gate

Three.js remains **RESEARCH CANDIDATE**, not a production dependency decision.

# 7. PHASE 1 DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The new experience should not be defined as:

```text
MORE ANIMATION
+ MORE 3D
+ MORE VISUAL EFFECTS
```

It should be defined as:

```text
CLEARER HUMAN ANCHOR
+ STRONGER MEDIA ARCHITECTURE
+ STRONGER SCENE COMPOSITION
+ PURPOSEFUL DEPTH
+ CONTINUOUS JOURNEY
+ FUNCTIONAL MOTION
+ RESPONSIVE INTENT
```

CASTRO’S should feel authored and memorable while remaining calm, professional and usable.

# 8. MOTION LEVELS FOR EXPERIENCE 2.0

Existing categories remain valid:

- ENTER
- EXIT
- REVEAL
- FOCUS
- SELECTION
- EXPANSION
- COLLAPSE
- CONTINUITY
- FEEDBACK
- PROGRESS
- SUCCESS
- ERROR

Implementation levels remain:

1. `MICRO` — controls, tabs, slots, buttons.
2. `OBJECT` — service/course/space gains focus.
3. `SCENE` — hierarchy and media enter/reframe.
4. `NARRATIVE` — research layer only; one meaningful anchor persists/reframes across related scenes.

NARRATIVE is not permission for aggressive scroll hijacking.

# 9. TYPOGRAPHY RULE DURING CONCEPT WORK

**No font-family change.**

Concept work must use:

```text
Instrument Serif → editorial/public headings
Manrope          → body/navigation/forms/controls/UI
```

What may be explored without changing the type system:

- scale;
- line length;
- line breaks;
- weight where the existing family supports it;
- contrast between display/editorial and supporting UI text;
- text/media overlap only when legible;
- reveal/kinetic timing;
- responsive hierarchy;
- composition around typography.

Any future proposal to replace either family requires a separate explicit user approval and brand-level rationale. It must never happen implicitly inside a redesign PR.

# 10. NEXT STORYBOARD GATE

The next creative deliverable should be **three scene storyboards**, not production code.

## A — HERO / HUMAN PRESENCE

Storyboard questions:

- what is the first visual anchor?
- where does real Elizabeth/environment media enter?
- how does Instrument Serif lead the composition without becoming a static title block?
- what persists into the next scene?
- what is the first meaningful action?
- how does mobile recompose it?

## B — TRAINING / ACTIVE KNOWLEDGE

Storyboard questions:

- how does learning feel active before registration?
- where can real workshop media live?
- how does the current real session data remain authoritative?
- what gains focus and why?
- how does the visitor move from discovery to a published session to registration?

## C — SPACES / SIGNATURE SPATIAL EXPERIENCE

Storyboard questions:

- can a simplified spatial representation orient the visitor?
- when should photography replace abstraction?
- how does 360 exploration connect to configuration?
- what should persist into availability/booking?
- what is the non-WebGL/mobile/reduced-motion equivalent?

# 11. IMPLEMENTATION GATE

Do **not** perform the large public redesign until:

1. Phase 1 research is accepted;
2. Hero / Training / Spaces storyboards are defined;
3. technology choice is justified per scene;
4. high-risk ideas (3D/narrative motion) are isolated in prototypes;
5. the user approves the concept direction;
6. production work is split into short-lived branches with normal CI discipline.

This file records research, not approval to implement the final visual experience.
